Problem Statement:
    Given an integer array arr of size n and two integers k1 and k2 (where k1 < k2), find the sum of all elements that lie strictly between the k1-th largest and the k2-th largest element in the array.
    The k1-th and k2-th largest elements themselves are excluded from the sum.

Input Format:
    First line: n space-separated integers (the array)
    Second line: integer k1
    Third line: integer k2

Output Format:
    A single integer: the sum of elements between the k1-th and k2-th largest

Constraints:
    1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
    1 ≤ k1 < k2 ≤ n
    1 ≤ arr[i] ≤ 10^6

Example 1:
    Input:
        12 14 10 2 5 18 9 11 3 20 8 6
        2
        5

    Output: 14

    Explanation:

        Sorted descending: 20, 18, 14, 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 6, 5, 3, 2

        Rank	    Element
        1st largest	20
        2nd largest	18 ← k1 (excluded)
        3rd largest	14
        4th largest	12
        5th largest	11 ← k2 (excluded)

        Sum between = 14 + 12 = 26

Example 2:

    Input:
        10 20 30 40 50
        1
        4

    Output: 70

    Explanation:

        Sorted descending: 50, 40, 30, 20, 10

        Rank	    Element
        1st largest	50 ← k1 (excluded)
        2nd largest	40
        3rd largest	30
        4th largest	20 ← k2 (excluded)

        Sum between = 40 + 30 = 70

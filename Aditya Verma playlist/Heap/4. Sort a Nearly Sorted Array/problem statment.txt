Problem Statement:
    Given an array arr[] of n distinct integers where each element is at most k positions away from its correctly sorted position, sort the array in ascending order.
    You must achieve O(n log k) time complexity and O(k) space complexity.

Constraints:

    1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
    1 ≤ k < n
    All elements are distinct
    Each element is at most k positions away from its sorted position

Input
    7 3
    6 5 3 2 8 10 9

First line: n (size of array) and k (max displacement)
Second line: n space-separated integers

Output
    2 3 5 6 8 9 10

    The sorted array as space-separated integers.

Examples
    #	Input	            Output
    1	7 36 5 3 2 8 10 9	2 3 5 6 8 9 10
    2	5 21 4 5 3 2	    1 2 3 4 5
    3	6 13 2 1 6 5 4	    1 2 3 4 5 6
    4	4 24 3 2 1	        1 2 3 4
